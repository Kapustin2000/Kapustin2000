# ASRP.science — Peer-Reviewed Scientific Journal

*2024 – Present · Active*

**Developed at:** [ASRP](../../experience/asrp/)

ASRP.science is an international peer-reviewed scientific journal publishing interdisciplinary research beyond conventional academic boundaries. Uses double-blind peer review to maintain academic integrity. Built on a custom CMS with AI-powered content organization.

**Live Website:** [https://asrp.science/en](https://asrp.science/en)

## Key Features

- **Double-Blind Peer Review** – Anonymous review process ensuring academic rigor
- **Interdisciplinary Scope** – Neuroscience, AI, consciousness studies, quantum computing, cryptobiology
- **Custom Publishing Platform** – Tailored CMS for manuscript submission, review, and publication workflows
- **Open Access** – Research freely available to the global scientific community

## My Role

- Architected and developed the publishing platform
- Implemented submission and peer review management workflows
- Integrated AI tools for content categorization and research analysis
- Designed scalable infrastructure for growing publication volume
