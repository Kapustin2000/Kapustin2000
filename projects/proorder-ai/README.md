# ProOrder AI — Email-to-ERP Order Automation

*December 2025 – March 2026 · Completed*

**Developed at:** [Osnova GmbH](../../experience/osnova/)

**Project Overview:**  
ProOrder AI automated order intake from inbound emails and connected the extracted business data to ERP workflows. The system focused on turning messy real-world email formats into structured orders with customer and product matching.

## Key Features

- **Email Order Extraction** – LLM-assisted parsing of inbound order emails
- **ERP Lookup Integration** – Customer and product matching through Business Central APIs
- **Format Resilience** – Support for code variations, blocked products, and multi-line tables
- **Operational Diagnostics** – Logging and debugging tooling for failed or ambiguous order cases
- **Production Workflow Support** – Deployment-ready runtime with monitoring-oriented troubleshooting paths

## My Role and Contributions

- Improved product search reliability with fallback logic for blocked or variant product codes
- Refined customer-recognition behavior for edge cases and messy real-world inputs
- Added unit-conversion and date-handling fixes to reduce downstream processing failures
- Built debugging and logging support used during production hardening and go-live preparation
- Focused on practical accuracy improvements rather than only model-side experimentation
